dancing.

Dance has always been a huge part of my life. The stage is where I feel most alive. Here are some of my favourite performances in London.

Selected events

Good Chance Theatre · Trafalgar Square

The Walk (Little Amal)

Little Amal, a giant puppet by Handspring Puppet Company, has travelled over 8,000km for displaced children. For her 10th birthday in London, 50+ volunteers rehearsed for four weeks. Choreography by Kenrick ‘H2O’ Sandy, music by Michael ‘Mikey J’ Asante.

Boy Blue · Buckingham Palace

Platinum Jubilee Pageant

The celebration of 70 years since Elizabeth II’s accession. A once-in-a-lifetime chance to perform with one of the UK’s most celebrated hip hop dance companies.

Fatima Robinson · Wembley Stadium

UEFA Champions League Final

Rock icon Lenny Kravitz headlined an unforgettable performance in London at the UEFA Champions League Final Kick Off Show presented by Pepsi.

Teaching · Uptop Dance School

Children's Instructor & Choreographer

I led two weekly hip-hop classes for dancers ages 4–5 and 6–9, building their foundational skills and choreographing original routines for the end-of-year recital. I also helped facilitate and lead a high-energy flash mob featuring the entire school.

Dance and code?

It’s a rare mix, and I’d love to use it. If you’re making a performance that needs a digital side, or a digital project that needs to move, let’s talk.
